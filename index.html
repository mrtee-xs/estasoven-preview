Customer Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my cake?

For multi-tier wedding cakes, we recommend booking at least 14 to 21 days in advance to reserve your date and allow time for custom floral sculpting. Milestone birthday cakes require 48 hours notice, while minimalist bento cakes can often be scheduled with 24 hours notice.

What payment terms apply to custom cake orders?

A 60% production deposit is required to confirm your order and reserve your kitchen baking slot. The remaining 40% balance is payable prior to dispatch or upon confirmation of finished cake photographs before delivery.

How should I store and serve my cake on the day of the event?

Because our cakes are crafted with pure butter, store the cake in an air-conditioned room (not in a damp refrigerator which can cause fondant sweating). Bring the cake to room temperature 45 minutes before slicing so the butter sponge softens to its intended melt-in-mouth texture.

Can I customize colors, design themes, and flavor combinations?

Yes. Every cake at Estasoven is custom-designed. After using our tier customizer, you can share reference swatches, party themes, or mood boards directly via our WhatsApp line (+234 806 749 6736), and our pastry artists will tailor the finish accordingly.
